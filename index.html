On-time4.29

Scale 0 to 5 · delivered Olist orders
  • Cleaned and validated 96,470 delivered orders across a 5-table relational dataset using Python and pandas; resolved null delivery dates and verified 10,000+ multi-unit line items were not duplicates.
  • Wrote advanced MySQL queries (CTEs, window functions: LAG, ROW_NUMBER, RANK) for monthly revenue trends, month-over-month growth, top categories and repeat-customer rate.
  • Found 8.1% of orders were delivered late and average review score was 4.29 for on-time orders vs 2.57 for late orders; review scores declined beyond 21 delivery days. Recommended a proactive delay-alert system.
  • Built a one-page Power BI dashboard with DAX measures (revenue, AOV, late %, average review); cross-validated total revenue (R$13.22M) across Python, SQL, Excel and Power BI.
